Party Rental Support

Party Rental™ is the rental business app: your gear, your bookings, your deliveries and pickups, and what you're owed, all in one place. Works with no signal.

Need help or found a bug?
Email jeff@loboz.com and include what you were doing and what you expected to happen. You'll usually hear back within a couple of days.

Common questions

How do I set up my inventory?

On the Assets tab, add each thing you rent: its name, how many you own (or each numbered unit for big items like inflatables), what it cost, its rate, and how long it needs between rentals for cleaning and drying. Companions that always travel with an item, like stakes and the blower for a bounce house, ride along automatically when you book it.

How does booking work?

A rental is two stops on your calendar: the delivery and the pickup. Pick the client, the dates, and the gear; the app checks every overlapping rental plus each asset's turnaround and warns you before you double-book. You can book over a warning, but you write down why.

What happens at pickup?

You count everything back in at the truck. A shortfall or damage gets a note on the asset's history, and the app offers a fee line you can charge or skip. Nothing is charged unless you put it there.

Is my data backed up?

Your work is saved on your device the instant you tap it, signal or not. If your device is signed into iCloud, Party Rental also backs up to your private iCloud automatically. Settings shows you plainly whether backup is on.

Can I get my data out?

Always. Settings → Export everything as CSV gives you plain files of your clients, stops, and money. The client file imports straight back into a fresh install, so your business is yours to take anywhere.

What does payback on an asset mean?

Each asset adds up what it has earned from its own line on every rental it went out on, against what you paid for it. When it crosses its cost, the screen says so, and everything after is running profit. If you never entered a cost, it says "no cost recorded" instead of guessing.

Privacy

Party Rental has no tracking and no ads. Your clients, money, and history stay on your device and in your private iCloud. Two optional features use a server if you switch them on, crew sign-in and card payments, and the privacy policy explains exactly what each one stores.
