User guide

Party Rental for iOS

Start with your gear

The Assets tab is your warehouse on paper. Add each thing you rent: a count for things like chairs and tables, or numbered units for big-ticket items like inflatables and tents, so each one keeps its own damage history. Give each asset its rate (flat per rental, or per day), what it cost you, and its turnaround, the days it needs between rentals for cleaning and drying.

Add your clients

On the Clients tab, add people by hand, pick them from Contacts, or import a CSV. Each client gets an address with access notes: the gate code, where to unload, where the power is. Those notes show up on the stop card on delivery day.

Book a rental

From the Schedule tab, Add booking. Pick the client, the delivery and pickup times, and the gear. Companions ride along automatically, and every line's price can be edited before you book. If something is already promised to another event that weekend, the app tells you exactly what and when, and booking over the warning requires a reason you write yourself.

Delivery day

The Schedule tab opens on today: your deliveries and pickups in order, with the address, the access notes, and a Directions button on each card. Completing the delivery is the money moment: the rental's charge lands on the client's balance, dated to that day. A Reminder button on each stop drafts the "we deliver tomorrow" text for you; you read it and send it from your own number.

Pickup day

Completing the pickup counts the load back in. Every line starts at its full count; if something is short or damaged, turn the count down and write what happened. The note lands on that asset's history, and a fee line is offered, never assumed.

Money

The Money tab is what you're owed and what you've earned: balances, aging, invoices, payments, tips, and your expenses. Invoices are PDFs generated on your device; send them however you like. If you connect Stripe, invoices can carry a pay link, and card money goes straight to your own Stripe account.

Crew (optional)

If people work your trucks, turn on crew in Settings. Each crew member signs into a simple web page with your join code, their name, and a PIN, and sees the day you assigned them: stops, addresses, and what to load. You choose what they can see; prices stay hidden unless you turn them on.

Every screen tells the truth

Empty screens say they're empty. Estimates say they're estimates. If a number could not be loaded, the screen says it could not check, instead of showing you a zero that isn't real.
