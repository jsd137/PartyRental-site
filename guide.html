User guide

Party Rental for iOS

Party Rental runs a small party rental business from your phone: the clients, the bookings on your calendar, the money, and the gear itself. The app has five tabs.

  • Schedule: every delivery and pickup, day by day.
  • Clients: who you rent to, with addresses and site details.
  • Assets: your rental catalog.
  • Money: charges, payments, expenses, and invoices.
  • Settings: message templates, crew access, card payments, and export.

Set up your catalog

Everything you rent out lives on the Assets tab. An asset is one of two kinds. A counted asset is a quantity you own, which fits chairs, tables, and linens. A serialized asset gives every unit its own id and its own damage history, which fits inflatables and other big-ticket items.

Each asset also carries:

  • Rate: a flat price per rental, or a price per day of the rental's span.
  • Rebooking buffer: the number of days after pickup before the asset can go out again, for drying and cleaning.
  • Acquisition cost: what you paid for it. The app measures earnings against this figure.

To add one:

  1. Open the Assets tab and start a new asset.
  2. Choose counted or serialized. For a counted asset, enter how many you own. For a serialized asset, add each unit with its own id.
  3. Set the rate, flat or per day.
  4. Enter the rebooking buffer in days.
  5. Enter what the asset cost you.

Open any asset later to see what it has earned against its cost, which of it is out right now, and its damage history.

Add your clients

The Clients tab holds the people you rent to. Add a client by hand, pull one in from Contacts, or import a CSV. A client record carries the address and the site details you want in hand on delivery day, such as the gate code and where to unload. Those details travel to the stop card when you book.

Book a rental

A booking pairs one client with a set of assets over a date range, and becomes two stops on the schedule: a delivery and a pickup.

  1. On the Schedule tab, start a new booking.
  2. Pick the client.
  3. Set the delivery date and time, then the pickup date and time.
  4. Add the assets as line items. Any line's price can be edited before you save.
  5. Save the booking.

If the dates collide with gear that is already promised, counting each asset's rebooking buffer, the app warns you before saving and names the booking in the way. To book over the warning, type a reason. The reason is saved with the booking.

Run the day

The Schedule tab opens on today, with the day's stops in the order you work them. Each stop card carries the address, the client's site details, and directions.

  1. Open the Schedule tab.
  2. Tap the stop you are working.
  3. Complete the delivery once the gear is set up.

Completing the delivery is the money moment. The booking's charge lands on the client's balance, dated to that day.

Check gear back in

Completing the pickup is check-in. Every line starts at the count that went out.

  1. Open the pickup stop on the Schedule tab.
  2. Count each asset back in. Turn the count down on any line that came back short.
  3. Write a damage note where one is needed. On a serialized asset the note lands on that unit's history.
  4. Add a fee line if you are charging for the shortfall or the damage. Fees are entered by hand at check-in.
  5. Complete the pickup. Each asset's rebooking buffer starts running from this date.

Get paid

The Money tab holds charges, payments, expenses, and invoices. Record a payment against a client's balance whenever it arrives. Build an invoice from what a client owes, send it, and mark it paid when the money lands.

For card payments, connect your Stripe account in Settings. Clients then pay from a link, and the money goes to your own Stripe account. Recording cash and checks is free.

Reminders

Party Rental keeps message templates for delivery reminders, pickup reminders, and payment due. Edit the wording in Settings so the texts sound like you.

  1. Open a booking, or open the run sheet for tomorrow's stops.
  2. Choose the reminder you want to send.
  3. The text opens in Messages with the client filled in. Read it and send it from your own number.

Crew

The crew day view is a web page a helper opens on their phone, showing the stops for that day. Set it up in Settings.

  1. In Settings, turn on crew access.
  2. Add each helper.
  3. Send them the link and their sign-in. They open it on their phone and see the day.

Your data

The app works with no signal. Everything you enter is written to the device as you enter it. When the device is signed into iCloud, your work syncs through your own iCloud account to your other devices, and Settings shows the current sync state.

Settings also exports your clients, stops, and money as CSV files. The client file imports back into a fresh install. What the app stores, and where, is set out in the privacy policy.
