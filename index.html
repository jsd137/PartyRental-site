Party Rental™

The party rental business app. Your gear, your bookings, your deliveries and pickups, and what you're owed, all in one place. Works with no signal.

  • An asset catalog: what you own, what it rents for, and where it is right now
  • Every rental is two calendar stops, the delivery and the pickup
  • Booking checks your inventory and warns before you double-book a Saturday
  • Count everything back in at the truck; shortfalls and damage get a note, and a fee only if you charge one
  • Payback per asset: what each bounce house has earned against what it cost
  • One-tap delivery and pickup reminder texts, sent from your own number
  • Invoices, payments, tips, and past-due tracking
  • Backs up to your private iCloud
  • Optional: give your crew their own sign-in, and take card payments
  • No ads, no tracking

Currently in private testing.
